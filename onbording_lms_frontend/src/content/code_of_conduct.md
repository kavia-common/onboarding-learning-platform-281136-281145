# Company Code of Conduct

Welcome to the team! This Code of Conduct outlines our shared values and expectations. Please read it carefully.

## Our Values
- Integrity and honesty in all interactions
- Respect for colleagues, customers, and partners
- Commitment to quality, safety, and compliance
- Inclusivity and zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination

## Professional Conduct
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations
- Protect confidential information
- Use company resources responsibly
- Communicate respectfully and collaborate constructively

## Security & Privacy
- Follow security best practices and training
- Report suspicious activity immediately
- Safeguard personal and company data

## Reporting & Enforcement
- If you witness misconduct, report it to HR or your manager
- Violations may result in corrective actions up to termination

By acknowledging below, you confirm you have read and agree to uphold this Code of Conduct.
